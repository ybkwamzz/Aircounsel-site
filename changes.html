UK-DELTA-SW-01 · SKILLED_WORKER · Statement of Changes HC 590 & 2025/2026 SOC 2020 Going-Rate Tables EFFECTIVE: 2024-04-04

Skilled Worker Visa £38,700 vs £29,000 Transitional Protection Rules (Before & After 4 April 2024)

BEFORE: Prior Rule (Pre-4 April 2024): General salary threshold of £26,200 per year (or £10.75/hr) based on the 25th percentile of ASHE earnings under SOC 2010 codes.
AFTER: Current Rule (Post-4 April 2024): General salary threshold raised to £38,700 per year (or the median 50th percentile SOC 2020 going rate, whichever is higher).
Transitional Cutoff (2024-04-04): Transitional Protection Rule: If you have held continuous Skilled Worker permission since BEFORE 4 April 2024 (and your first CoS was assigned before 4 April 2024), the lower transitional general threshold (£29,000 or 25th percentile going rate) applies when extending or changing sponsors until 4 April 2030.
What to prepare: Prepare your 4-digit SOC 2020 occupation code, weekly contracted hours (for pro-rata going-rate calculation), and initial CoS assignment date.

UK-DELTA-FM-01 · SPOUSE · Statement of Changes HC 590 — Appendix FM Family Route EFFECTIVE: 2024-04-11

UK Spouse & Partner Visa £29,000 vs £18,600 Minimum Income Transitional Rules (Appendix FM)

BEFORE: Prior Rule (Pre-11 April 2024): Minimum gross annual income of £18,600 (plus child add-ons), or £62,500 in Category D cash savings.
AFTER: Current Rule (Post-11 April 2024): Minimum gross annual income of £29,000 for new first-time applicants on the partner route (no child add-on), or £88,500 in cash savings held for 6 months.
Transitional Cutoff (2024-04-11): Transitional Protection Rule: Applicants who submitted their initial 5-year partner route application BEFORE 11 April 2024 and are applying to extend or settle (ILR) with the same partner remain assessed under the £18,600 threshold.
What to prepare: Organise 6 months (Category A) or 12 months (Category B) of bank statements, payslips, and employer letter matching Appendix FM-SE rules.

UK-DELTA-EVISA-01 · ILR · UKVI Digital Immigration Status (eVisa) & Carrier Verification Guidance EFFECTIVE: 2025-01-01

UK eVisa & Expired BRP Card Rules: Linking Your Passport & Generating a Share Code

BEFORE: Prior Rule: Physical Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) cards with a printed 31 December 2024 card expiry date used for travel and right-to-work checks.
AFTER: Current Rule: Physical BRPs are replaced by a digital UKVI Account and eVisa linked to the holder's current passport. Airlines and employers verify status digitally.
Transitional Cutoff (2025-01-01): Travel & Passport Renewal Rule: Expiry of the physical BRP card does not expire your underlying immigration leave, but you must link any new passport number to your UKVI account before international travel.
What to prepare: Verify UKVI account login, confirm current passport number linkage, and generate a 9-character Share Code.

UK-DELTA-SPON-01 · SPONSOR_LICENCE · Workers and Temporary Workers: Guidance for Sponsors Part 2 & Part 3 EFFECTIVE: 2025-04-01

UK Sponsor Licence Changes: 10-Year Auto-Extension, Fee Recoupment Ban & Digital Audits

BEFORE: Prior Rule: Manual 4-year Sponsor Licence renewal applications and less explicit prohibitions on clawback clauses for sponsorship fees.
AFTER: Current Rule: Routine 4-year renewals replaced by 10-year automatic extensions, paired with strict licence revocation triggers if a sponsor passes or recoups Sponsor Licence or CoS fees from a sponsored worker.
Transitional Cutoff (2025-01-01): Compliance Audit Trigger: Digital HMRC PAYE cross-checking and unannounced UKVI compliance audits require sponsors to maintain complete Appendix D recruitment and salary records.
What to prepare: Prepare your Appendix D dossier, Authorising Officer SMS log, and employment contract fee-clause audit before requesting Defined or Undefined CoS.
