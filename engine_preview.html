The Six Qualification Gates

  1. 01

    Route Fit

    Is the enquiry consistent with a viable UK immigration route for this individual?

  2. 02

    Eligibility Thresholds

    Does the enquiry meet current Home Office salary, qualification, and residency requirements?

  3. 03

    Immigration History

    Are there flags — refusals, overstays, prior applications — that affect viability?

  4. 04

    Fee Capacity

    Can the enquiry realistically proceed as a privately funded matter at your firm's fee level?

  5. 05

    Jurisdiction

    Is this a matter that falls within UK immigration jurisdiction and your firm's remit?

  6. 06

    Urgency

    Is there a time-sensitive element — an expiry, a court date, a removal notice — that affects prioritisation?

Why This Matters to Managers

The problem is not volume.
It is the cost of the wrong volume.

"We don't need more enquiries — we need clarity."

Every non-viable enquiry that reaches a solicitor’s desk costs time that cannot be recovered. As an illustration, at a partner rate of £200 per hour and 0.75 partner hours per unqualified enquiry, five unqualified enquiries a week comes to roughly 195 hours, or £39,000, of partner time a year, before a single case is opened. Your own figures will differ.

Layer 1 of the AirCounsel engine gives practice managers a mechanism to protect that time. It does not change how your team works. It changes what reaches them.

The pilot requires no technical setup, no system integration, and no preparation from your staff. You see the output in 10 minutes. You decide whether it works for your firm.

Simple, Non-Technical

Five stages.
One controlled intake window.

Stage 01

Capture

Record the enquiry and the information supplied at the point of first contact.

Stage 02

Structure

Arrange declared information into consistent fields, preserving what is supplied and what remains missing.

Stage 03

Score

Apply configured intake checks and record the resulting indicators. A score is a workflow signal, not a legal conclusion.

Stage 04

Prioritise

Classify the enquiry for staff attention. Not Viable does not mean automatic rejection or client-facing redirection.

Stage 05

Deliver

Present a structured summary and its outstanding checks to the authorised team for review and next-step control.

This is a product illustration, not a deployment assurance. Integration scope, data access, retention, staff responsibilities and operational controls must be agreed with the firm before any real enquiry data is introduced.
