1. Core System Capabilities & Architectural Invariants

1.1 Propose-Only AI Boundary

Deterministic code decides; AI only proposes. Principals authenticated with ActorKind.LLM_AGENT may attach structured field-extraction proposals (v2_ev_agent_proposals) but are structurally barred at the database kernel from calling record_human_review() or release_document().

1.2 Byte-Pinned Human Attestation

Before any evidence transitions to RELEASED in Trusted storage, an authenticated human practitioner (ActorKind.HUMAN, role fee_earner or senior_reviewer) must attest to the exact SHA-256 digest (expected_sha256 == version.sha256). Uploading v2 atomically supersedes v1 and invalidates prior sign-off.

1.3 55-Gate Deterministic Intake Sanitiser

Inbound web and voice enquiries pass through 55 deterministic checks (blocking prompt injection, SSRF, template injection, null bytes, and homoglyphs) and evaluate 6 practice qualification gates (Route Fit, Eligibility, Immigration History, Fee Capacity, Jurisdiction, and Section 3C Urgency).

1.4 Sharded HMAC Audit & Sensory Organ

State transitions and authorised voice/vision commands emit Zero-PII pointer events chained by (tenant_id, case_id, case_seq), signed via keyed HMAC-SHA256 (ExternalKMSAnchor), and protected by database BEFORE UPDATE/DELETE immutability triggers.

3. Methodology for Modelled Practice Efficiency Estimates

References in our institutional briefings to an estimated ~22% uninstructed consultation rate and 6–10 hours per week of recoverable fee-earner capacity are modelled practice estimates derived from the following operational parameters:

  • Uninstructed Consultation Drag (~22% Modelled Estimate): Models a specialist immigration practice handling 25 inbound enquiries per week in which 5 to 6 consultations do not convert to instruction due to pre-existing statutory blockers (e.g., Appendix Skilled Worker going-rate shortfall, absence of a Home Office Sponsor Licence, or undisclosed adverse immigration history).
  • Recoverable Fee-Earner Capacity (6–10 Hours/Week Modelled Estimate): Models 5.5 uninstructed consultations/week × 45 minutes (4.1 hours) plus 12 active matters × 25 minutes/week of manual document chasing and pre-supervision verification (5.0 hours) = ~9.1 hours/week per fee earner at an illustrative cost rate of £275/hour.

4. Onboarding Verification Gate & Synthetic Data Restriction

To safeguard special-category personal data (UK GDPR Article 9) and criminal-offence data (Article 10):

  • Stage 1 | Self-Serve Sandbox Provisioning (/onboard): Mints a Synthetic-Data Sandbox Key (sk_sandbox_..., status SANDBOX_PROVISIONED_SRA_PENDING) verified against our database on every call and rate-limited per IP. Unverified tenants are restricted to synthetic test payloads.
  • Stage 2 | Production Activation: Live production credentials for client PII are issued only after verification of the practice's SRA Number / IAA (OISC) Registration / Companies House Number and execution of our UK GDPR Article 28 Data Processing Addendum.
