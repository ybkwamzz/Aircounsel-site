2. Directive Error Code Reference Manual (What Went Wrong & How to Fix It)

Every operational block in AirCounsel STRATA returns a deterministic reference code so applicants and practitioners know the exact corrective step:

Error CodeCause & Operational ContextDirective Action (What To Do Now)
AC-ERR-101
Applicant		 Enquiry Preparation Mandate Unsigned (CONTRACT-PUB-01)
You attempted to route a Preparation Record to a practice before confirming the Non-Advice & Data-Sharing Consent Mandate.		 What to do: Tick the 'Confirm Factual Preparation & Single-Practice Sharing Consent' box in Step 04 and click Route Record again.
AC-ERR-102
Applicant		 Input Blocked by 55-Gate Security Sanitiser
A text field in your enquiry contained formatting characters resembling code, scripts, or external server links.		 What to do: Remove any URLs, angle brackets (< >), or code symbols from your free-text chronology and resubmit.
AC-ERR-201
Solicitor / Practice		 Practice Regulator Licence or Liability Contracts Pending
Your practice is currently in Synthetic Sandbox Mode because SRA/IAA register verification or CONTRACT-FIRM-01 / CONTRACT-DPA-01 signature is incomplete.		 What to do: Complete SRA/IAA licence verification and sign both canonical agreements via /onboard or POST /api/v1/contracts/sign.
AC-ERR-202
Solicitor / Practice		 Golden Matrix Role Capability Denied (Propose-Only / Read-Only Actor)
An LLM Agent (ActorKind.LLM_AGENT), Coordinator, or Read-Only Auditor attempted to attest or release client evidence.		 What to do: Switch your active Principal in the workspace topbar to SOLICITOR (fee_earner) or PARTNER (senior_reviewer) before attesting evidence.
AC-ERR-301
Solicitor / Practice		 Supersession Sign-Off Invalidation
A newer document version (v2) was uploaded after v1 was approved, automatically invalidating v1's attestation.		 What to do: Open the 3-Axis Split-Pane Inspector on the latest version (v2) and record a fresh byte-pinned SHA-256 attestation.
AC-ERR-302
Solicitor / Practice		 UK GDPR Article 9 / Privilege Precondition Unresolved at Handoff
Supervision handoff was attempted while Special-Category Data had art9_condition set to NONE_REQUIRED or Privilege set to PENDING.		 What to do: Select a valid UK GDPR Article 9(2) condition (e.g., Legal Claims) and complete the Legal Professional Privilege review before handoff.
AC-ERR-401
Both (Applicant & Practice)		 Primary Practice Declined (Conflict or Capacity) | Access Revoked
The selected practice declined the introduction due to a conflict of interest or capacity limit. Their access token has been permanently revoked.		 What to do: If you pre-selected a Backup Practice, your record has already been routed to Choice #2 automatically. Otherwise, select another online practice.
